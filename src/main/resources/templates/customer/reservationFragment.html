{! The booking's live state: seats, their statuses, and the payment card. Polled by htmx while a QRIS charge is outstanding, so a customer who has scanned the QR sees their seats go confirmed without pressing anything. Before this, the page offered a manual "Refresh" link and the copy below promised confirmation "automatically" — which was true of the booking and false of the page. WHY THE WHOLE FRAGMENT AND NOT JUST THE PAYMENT CARD: settlement changes both halves at once. The payment becomes paid AND every seat goes held -> confirmed, so swapping only the payment card would leave the table above it contradicting it. WHY THE POLLING ATTRIBUTES ARE CONDITIONAL: once the payment is terminal this fragment renders without hx-get/hx-trigger, htmx has nothing left to schedule, and the polling stops of its own accord. No JavaScript decides when to stop, and nothing has to remember to cancel a timer — the same markup-drives-behaviour principle the seat map's revert relies on. A stuck poll would otherwise run for as long as the tab stayed open. A failed poll is cosmetic, not a correctness problem. Settlement is applied by the BI-SNAP notification server-side; this page only reports it. If the poll 401s because an OIDC session expired mid-wait, the seats are still confirmed — the customer just will not see it until they reload. !}
