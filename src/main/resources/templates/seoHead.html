{! The head markup a public page contributes, in one place rather than repeated seven times. Included from inside each page's {#head} block — see base.html's {#insert head}. Every line is conditional on purpose. With no configured public origin (local development, and the whole test suite) `meta.canonicalUrl` is null and the page emits no canonical and no og:url — which is the honest thing to say when the deployment does not know its own address, and better than pointing a crawler at localhost. The JSON-LD is rendered with .raw, and that is safe ONLY because SeoMarkup built it: the string has already been through Jackson and then had <, > and & rewritten to their \u00XX forms, so no film title an operator types can close this script element. Do not put a template expression of your own inside the script block — Qute HTML-escapes it, which emits ' into raw-text content and breaks the JSON, and reaching for .raw to fix that is the script-injection hole JsonLd exists to close. Add the value to SeoMarkup instead. !} {#if meta.canonicalUrl} {/if} {!
